## Principal Engineer take home test:

We would like you to put together a high level architecture document proposing a solution for one of the two
scenarios presented below:

1. An airport trip planner, that offers recommendations on restaurants and shops I could visit based on my
   preferences, my flight time and potential sales happening at shops.
  - Assume we have:
    1. A service that will provide you with my selected preferences and flight time
    2. A service that provides a list of shops and restaurants in the airport along with some keywords that
describe the shop - these keywords are not standardised and provided by the brands themselves 
      - Eg: McDonalds - fast food, cheap, takeaway 
	  - Coach - luxury, fashion, service

2. A tool to predict airport lounge occupancy, helping our users choose which is the best lounge (or flight) to book
  - Assume we have:
    1. A service that provides real time indication of who has entered a lounge but not if they have left
    2. A service that provides my preferences and flight time
    3. A service that provides all the flights to and from the airport

### Outputs:

- A diagram presenting your back end, front end and infrastructure technology choices.
- A brief description on why each technology was chosen.
- The strengths and trade offs in your design.
